Welcome to CPDB

Welcome to the Cyclic Peptide DataBank (CPDB), a comprehensive knowledge base focused on the three-dimensional structures of cyclic peptides containing disulfide bonds.

Cyclic peptides, especially those with disulfide bonds, are at the forefront of biological research and drug discovery due to their unique stability, high target affinity, and potent biological activity. Our database aims to consolidate this vital structural information, which is often scattered across various sources, into a single, accessible platform.

A Foundation of Quality Data

Our database is built upon high-quality experimental data. The majority of structures are determined by X-ray diffraction, ensuring atomic-level precision, while a significant portion of our entries feature the key disulfide bonds characteristic of the peptides we focus on.

Core Features

  • Comprehensive Data Collection: Systematically integrates cyclic peptide structures with disulfide bonds from PDB and literature.
  • Precise Structural Annotation: Provides fine-grained annotations for each entry, including sequence, disulfide bond connectivity, and source.
  • Powerful Search System: Offers multi-dimensional search functions to quickly locate molecules of interest.
  • Interactive 3D Visualization: Embedded with advanced molecular visualization tools for interactive exploration.
  • Convenient Data Download: All structural data and related information support one-click download in various common formats.

Diversity and Precision

Data quality is paramount, with a large number of structures boasting high resolution. These peptides originate from a diverse range of species, many of which are known for producing potent bioactive compounds.
