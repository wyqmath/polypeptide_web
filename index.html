About Our Cyclic Peptide DataBank (CPDB)

Welcome to the Cyclic Peptide DataBank (CPDB) - a comprehensive knowledge base focused on the three-dimensional structures of cyclic peptides containing disulfide bonds.

Cyclic peptides, especially those containing disulfide bonds, have become a frontier in modern biological research and a key source for innovative drug development due to their unique structural stability, high target affinity, and excellent biological activity. However, the 3D structural information of these complex molecules is scattered across a vast amount of literature and multiple databases, posing a significant challenge for systematic research.

To address this challenge, we created CPDB. This database is dedicated to systematically collecting, organizing, and displaying high-precision 3D structural data of cyclic peptides containing at least one disulfide bond. We hope to break down data barriers and provide a comprehensive, accurate, intuitive, and easily accessible platform for cyclic peptide structural information for computational biologists, structural biologists, medicinal chemists, and researchers in related fields worldwide.

Core Features and Functions of this Database:

  • Comprehensive Data Collection: Systematically integrates cyclic peptide structures with disulfide bonds from the Protein Data Bank (PDB) and published literature, providing detailed source information and cross-reference links.
  • Precise Structural Annotation: Provides fine-grained biological and chemical annotations for each entry, including sequence, molecular weight, disulfide bond connectivity, source species, biological activity, and related target information.
  • Powerful Search System: Offers multi-dimensional, advanced search functions based on keywords, sequences, molecular formulas, sources, or targets to help users quickly locate molecules of interest.
  • Interactive 3D Visualization: Embedded with advanced molecular visualization tools, allowing users to interactively rotate, zoom, and highlight disulfide bonds and different residues in the 3D structure directly on the webpage.
  • Convenient Data Download: All structural data and related information support one-click download in various common formats (e.g., PDB, MOL2, SDF) to seamlessly integrate with mainstream molecular modeling and drug design software.

We believe that CPDB is not just a data repository but a catalyst for stimulating innovation and accelerating research. Whether you are dedicated to exploring new natural products or designing the next generation of peptide drugs, this database will provide you with valuable structural information support.
